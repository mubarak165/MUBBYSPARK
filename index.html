SECTION 07 / PHILOSOPHY

HOW I THINK ABOUT AN EDIT.

Every cut is intentional. Every transition serves the retention curve. Here is the framework behind every project that leaves my editing room.

01
STEP 01

HOOK

Give them an undeniable reason to stay. Eliminate all fluff at the start and drop straight into the core value or curiosity gap.

02
STEP 02

PACING

Cut the parts that don't earn attention. Breathe life into breaths, pauses, and dead air without making it feel rushed.

03
STEP 03

STORY

Make every second move the idea forward. Structure the narrative so the viewer's curiosity compounds until the end.

04
STEP 04

VISUALS

Make the message easier to understand with dynamic captions, sound effects, punch-in zooms, and relevant B-roll.

05
STEP 05

PAYOFF

Give the viewer a satisfying conclusion or a call-to-action that feels earned, not forced.
